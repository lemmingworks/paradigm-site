Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Paradigm French ("the app") is made by Lemmingworks. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Your progress stays on your device

Your scores, settings and progress history are stored only on your phone. There is no account and no sign-in, and we do not have a copy of your progress. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional unlock is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether the unlock has been bought, so that it can turn on the extra tenses. We never see your payment details.

Crash reports

If the app has an error, it may send a crash report to Sentry, our error reporting service, so that we can fix it. A report contains technical details only: the error, the app version, and the phone model and Android version. It does not contain your name, email, IP address, progress or any other personal information. Crash reports are deleted within 90 days.

Speech

Spoken answers use your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

No ads or tracking

The app has no advertising and no analytics, and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
